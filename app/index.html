Our method

PROPOSED PIPELINE / MEASURED OUTCOME
What this project adds

Refine the quantized projection, not just the precision.

Instead of stopping at direct rounding, the proposed pipeline starts with an INT8 PCA matrix and searches each coefficient at adjacent integer values. It keeps changes that reduce a combined reconstruction and orthogonality objective.

  1. 01
    Learn the projectionStandardize the inputs and fit PCA to retain 95% of training variance.
  2. 02
    Quantize and refineScale components by 128 into INT8, then run two local-search passes over up to 20 components.
  3. 03
    Classify in compact spaceProject samples with the refined matrix and train the 100-tree Random Forest.
